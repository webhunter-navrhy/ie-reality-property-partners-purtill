Property Partners Purtill are thrilled to present Apartment 21, Killegland Meadows.

A bright and airy owner occupied, three bedroom apartment, in well maintained condition, located on the ground floor of this established and popular development in the heart of Ashbourne.

No. 21 benefits from two balconies over looking the park and is ready for immediate occupancy.

Spacious accommodation comprises a large, open-plan kitchen/ dining room, coat closet, three bedrooms (master bedroom with en-suite) and a family bathroom completes the look for this move-in ready home.

Located centrally in Ashbourne, Killegland is an exceptional location, just a short stroll to Main street and equal distance to the park and playground. Killegland offers a private underground carpark with lift-access making this an ideal investment for those looking for close proximity to Dublin and access to all major road networks such as the M2, N2. Ashbourne offers a range of facilities such as schools, restaurants, bars, hotel, cinema, bowling, Emerald Park and much more.
